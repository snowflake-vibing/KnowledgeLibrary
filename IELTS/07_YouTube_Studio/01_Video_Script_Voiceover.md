`[Click: Block 1]`

`[Switch to Slide 19]`

"The opposite applies to the preposition **TO**: `[pause 1s]`

`[Click: Block 1]`
Use **TO** when a feature lies **SEPARATED and OUTSIDE the boundary** of the area.  
For example: *A dense forest lies TO the north of the town.*  
Or: *To the east of the river stands a modern railway station.*"

#### 🟢 SLIDE 25: 🔄 07. VERB GROUP 2: DEMOLITION & REMOVAL

#### 🟢 SLIDE 26: 🔄 07. VERB GROUP 3: REPLACEMENT & CONVERSION

### SECTION 8: 4 ADVANCED GRAMMAR STRUCTURES FOR BAND 7.5+

#### 🟢 SLIDE 28: 💡 08. ADVANCED GRAMMAR #1: PARTICIPLE CLAUSES
`[Switch to Slide 28]`

"In Section 8, let's explore **4 advanced grammar structures to elevate your GRA score**. `[pause 1s]`

`[Click: Block 1]`
Structure 1 is the **Passive Participle Clause**: Instead of writing two disjointed simple sentences, reduce the spatial clause to the front: *Located or Situated + [Location], [Main Subject] was + [Verb].* `[pause 1s]`

`[Click: Block 2]`
Exam model: *Located to the east of the primary road, the local school was significantly extended.* Fluid, concise, and academically sophisticated."

---

#### 🟢 SLIDE 29: 💡 08. ADVANCED GRAMMAR #2: SPATIAL INVERSION
`[Switch to Slide 29]`

"Structure 2 creates immediate stylistic impact: **Spatial Prepositional Inversion**. `[pause 1s]`

`[Click: Block 1]`
The rule is simple: Front the entire prepositional phrase of place **without needing any auxiliary verb**: *[Prepositional Phrase of Place] + stands, lies, or sat + [Subject].* `[pause 1s]`

`[Click: Block 2]`
From our library floor plan: *In the top-right corner of the library now stands a newly constructed lecture room.* Notice how naturally the subject follows the verb."

---

#### 🟢 SLIDE 30: 💡 08. ADVANCED GRAMMAR #3: AT THE EXPENSE OF
`[Switch to Slide 30]`

"Structure 3 is the **Trade-off relationship using 'at the expense of'**: `[pause 1s]`

`[Click: Block 1]`
This structure highlights that the expansion of one feature occurred at the sacrifice or reduction of another, reflecting deep analytical thought. `[pause 1s]`

`[Click: Block 2]`
Stokeford model: *The retirement home was expanded at the expense of the surrounding gardens.* A hallmark of Band 8.0+ writing."

---

#### 🟢 SLIDE 31: 💡 08. ADVANCED GRAMMAR #4: NOMINALIZATION WITNESSED
`[Switch to Slide 31]`

"Structure 4 is **Nominalization paired with personified verbs like 'witnessed' or 'saw'**: `[pause 1s]`

`[Click: Block 1]`
Instead of monotonous 'There was' patterns, appoint the location or timeframe as your sentence subject: *[Place or Time] + witnessed or saw + the [Noun Phrase of Change].* `[pause 1s]`

`[Click: Block 2]`
Model sentence: *The bottom-right corner witnessed the conversion of the enquiry desk into a café.* Active, vivid, and native-sounding."
